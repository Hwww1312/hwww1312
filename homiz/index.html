Our story

Born between bluestone and spray paint

Homiz started in Melbourne's laneways, where the walls change overnight and nobody waits for permission.

02

Feel Blessed, on the chest

Gratitude goes up front. The script is cracked and distressed, printed over an acid mineral wash so it already looks lived in.

03

Fear Less, on the back

Nerve goes behind you, with “i'm busy” stitched on the hood for anyone who tries to slow you down.

Made to be touched

Three raised puff stars down each sleeve. Heavy fleece, ribbed cuffs, a boxy cropped fit. No two washes come out the same.
