Returns & exchanges

Change of mind

If it's not right, you can return unworn, unwashed items with tags attached within 30 days of delivery for a refund to your original payment method or store credit. Return postage is paid by you. Sale items are final sale for change of mind.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Email us with your order number and the size you want, and we'll hold it while your return travels back.

Faulty or damaged items

If something arrives faulty, damaged, or not as described, email us photos within a reasonable time and we'll fix it, replace it or refund you, and cover the return postage.

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

How to start a return

Email us with your order number and the item. We'll reply with the return address and next steps within 2 business days.

Care

Acid-wash fleece keeps its marbling best with a gentle routine.

  • Wash cold, inside out, with similar colours. The first few washes may release some dye.
  • Don't bleach, and don't iron directly on the print or puff stars.
  • Line dry in the shade, or tumble dry on low.
  • Confirm fabric composition and weight with your manufacturer, e.g. 100% cotton, 450 gsm.

Privacy policy

Last updated [launch date]. This policy explains how Homiz (“we”) handles your personal information, consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, and delivery and billing addresses when you place an order.
  • Your email address when you join the Homiz Club.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, through cookies and analytics.

We never see or store your full card details. Payments are processed by Shopify Payments and other payment providers you choose at checkout.

How we use it

To process and deliver orders, handle returns and enquiries, send marketing emails you've agreed to, and understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the store: Shopify (our store platform and checkout), our payment providers, Australia Post (delivery) and Klaviyo (email). Some of these store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We don't sell your information.

Marketing emails

We only send marketing emails if you've opted in. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and we'll honour it promptly.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or raise a privacy concern, by emailing us. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
